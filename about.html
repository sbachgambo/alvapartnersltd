About Alva Partners

A trusted partner for consultancy, project management, and training since 2013—building partnerships that work, and now also an official NASENI Devfrontier partner for renewable energy.

Who We Are

ALVA PARTNERS LIMITED is a distinguished private limited liability company, registered under the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Established in 2013, we were founded with a vision of delivering tailored business solutions that help our clients navigate complex challenges and risks.

Over the years, we have built a reputation for integrity, excellence, and results—serving government agencies, private sector leaders, and development partners across Nigeria. Above all, we are known for building partnerships that work—across consultancy, project management, procurement, and training.

In 2026, we embarked on a strategic expansion of our services—becoming an official sales partner for NASENI Devfrontier Green Energy FZE. This adds renewable energy to our capabilities as a growing practice, while our consultancy, project management, and training services remain at the core of what we do.
